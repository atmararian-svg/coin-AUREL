# Brag Plan: Arian Atmar — Portfolio (arianatmar.com)

## What is this app?
A minimalist, white, Inter-set portfolio for Arian Atmar — "An Award-Winning Senior UI/UX Designer working with studios around the World" — showing six selected works and 12+ awards (Awwwards, FWA, CSSDA).

## The angle
Treat a designer's portfolio like a product launch. Borrow the kinetic-type grammar of the reference film (ObiN Studio's "Numtera" launch: one giant word per beat, dark→accent gradient fill on the type, soft blur-ins, hard background flips, UI close-ups) and apply it to the site's own design language: pure white field, Inter, near-black type, one red-orange accent `#ff2f00`, and the site's signature "Arian ——— Atmar" line intro. The type does the bragging; the real project cards prove it.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
"Meet" — a single enormous word blurs in and scales down to center with a near-black → `#ff2f00` gradient fill (the reference film's opener, re-coloured to the site's accent). Hard swap to the site's actual intro: "Arian" and "Atmar" pushed apart by a line drawing out from the center.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The about-page claim building word by word: "An **Award-Winning** / Senior UI/UX Designer" — accent on "Award-Winning", exactly like the site highlights words in `#ff2f00`.
- The homepage "selected works" grid: six real project cards (001 Razed Mods, 002 SŌM Power, 003 SŌM, 004 TPL, 005 Kanoi, 006 The Luxe Week) dealt in one by one on the beat, the giant faint "selected works" wordmark behind them, as on the site.
- Match-cut: SŌM Power card scales to fill the frame → "12+ AWARDS" counts up over it, then three award lines stack: Awwwards · Site of the Day / FWA · FWA of the Day / CSSDA · Website of the Day.
- Background flips back to white: "6+ years" and "30+ major projects" slam in on the two strongest music hits.

## Outro / punchline
The "Arian ——— Atmar" lockup lands on a strong beat, with "Specializing in UI/UX & web design" and `arianatmar.com` (accent) beneath. Hold.

## User flow worth showing
none — portfolio / landing-page site. Closest thing to a flow: landing intro → selected-works grid → about/recognition. The video follows that order.

## Tone
- Preset: app-store
- Creative direction: Numtera-style kinetic-type launch film (dynamic text movement) applied to a minimalist award-winning designer portfolio
- Interpretation: clean and premium, but the type is always moving — blur-ins, scale-downs, masked rises, slams, gradient sweeps; fast entrances, honest holds; no clutter, one accent colour.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080, 30fps
## Duration: 23s

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#ffffff` (site `--background`), with `#0d0d0d` / `#0a0a0a` dark flip for the awards beat
- Accent: `#ff2f00`
- Text: `#0d0d0d` (primary), `#c5c5c5` (muted grey, used for "(6+)"-style asides)
- Display font: Inter (tight negative tracking, as on the site)
- Body font: Inter
- Strongest visual element: the "Arian ——— Atmar" line intro and the dark laptop-mockup project cards against the white page

## Share copy (draft)
My portfolio, but make it a launch film. 6+ years, 30+ projects, 12+ awards — arianatmar.com

## Audio direction
- Role: warm upbeat bed with a light, consistent SFX layer
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (most energetic, 120 BPM)
- Music treatment: start at 0, bed at ~0.35, fade out over the last ~1.5s under the held lockup
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset read (`cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.json`), tempo 120.19 BPM, beats every ~0.5s on x.02/x.52. Strong cues to lock: 16.02s ("6+ years" slam), 17.02s ("30+ major projects" slam), 20.02s (lockup line lands). Beat-grid windows: word build 3.02–4.52 (one word-group per beat, then hold), card deal 7.02–9.52 (one card per beat — cards are images with tiny labels, the full grid then holds ≥1.5s), award lines 13.52/14.02/14.52 then hold.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; music RMS/bass makes a soft `#ff2f00` radial glow behind the type breathe. No waveform/equalizer visuals.
- SFX posture: moderate, motion-matched, light volume (0.55–0.75)
- Audio-coupled moments: hook word blur-in, card deal (card-slide/place), counter tick, stat slams (soft impact), final lockup (bell)
- Restraint rule: no glitch/error/punch sounds; never more than one SFX per beat.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook — 2.5s (0.0–2.5)
White field, faint warm glow. "Meet" — huge (≈380px), gradient-filled #0d0d0d→#ff2f00 — blurs in and scales down to center (0.1–0.6s), holds. At 1.4s hard swap to the site intro: "Arian" ——— "Atmar", the line drawing out from the center and pushing the two words apart.
Sequential/interaction: yes — word swap, then line draw
Audio intent: confident open
Audio-coupled idea: soft whoosh/drop on "Meet", light click on the swap
Music: upbeat bed from 0
Transition mood: hard → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The claim — 4.0s (2.5–6.5)
Kinetic sentence build, verbatim from the about page: line 1 "An Award-Winning" (Award-Winning in #ff2f00, italic), line 2 "Senior UI/UX Designer". Word groups rise through a mask on consecutive beats (3.02, 3.52, 4.02, 4.52), then the full sentence holds ≈1.8s. A gradient sweep runs through "Award-Winning" during the hold.
Sequential/interaction: yes — 4 word-groups on beats, then hold
Audio intent: momentum
Audio-coupled idea: tiny tick on first and last word-group only
Transition mood: clean (push/blur) → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Selected works — 5.5s (6.5–12.0)
The homepage grid: giant faint "selected works" wordmark low in frame; six real project cards (images from the site) deal in left→right on beats 7.02…9.52, each with its number (001…006), year ('26/'25/'24) and name. Full grid holds and slowly pushes in. At ~11.3s card 002 SŌM Power scales up to fill the frame (match-cut into Scene 4).
Sequential/interaction: yes — 6 cards one per beat, then ≥1.5s hold of the full set
Audio intent: playful, tactile
Audio-coupled idea: card-slide/place on first, middle and last card
Transition mood: scale-up match cut → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Recognition — 4.0s (12.0–16.0)
Dark field: SŌM Power image full-bleed, darkened and blurred. "12+" counts up 0→12 with "AWARDS" beside it (12.1–13.0). Then three lines stack on beats 13.52/14.02/14.52: "Awwwards · Site of the Day", "FWA · FWA of the Day", "CSSDA · Website of the Day" — then hold.
Sequential/interaction: yes — counter, then 3 lines one per beat, set holds ≈1.4s
Audio intent: payoff, weight
Audio-coupled idea: chip/tick on count-up end, soft drop on first award line
Transition mood: hard flip to white → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Numbers — 3.0s (16.0–19.0)
White again. "6+ years" slams in (scale+blur) on 16.02 (beat-locked); "30+ major projects" side-snaps in on 17.02 (beat-locked), "30+ major projects" in #ff2f00 as on the about page. Hold together.
Sequential/interaction: yes — two slams on strong cues
Audio intent: punch
Audio-coupled idea: soft medium impact on each slam
Transition mood: scale-swap → Scene 6

### Scene 6 — Lockup — 4.0s (19.0–23.0)
"Arian ——— Atmar" lockup: words arrive from the sides, the line draws in and lands at 20.02 (beat-locked). Under it: "Specializing in UI/UX & web design" (grey, small caps) and "arianatmar.com" in #ff2f00. Holds; music fades out.
Sequential/interaction: lockup assembles, then hold
Audio intent: resolve
Audio-coupled idea: one bell on the lockup landing
Transition mood: hold to end

**Music mood for this video:** upbeat
**Audio summary:** an upbeat 120-BPM bed carries the cuts, light card/tick accents follow the kinetic type, two soft impacts hit the stat slams, one bell lands the lockup, and the bed fades under the final hold.
